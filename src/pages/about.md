I'm an Architect Scrum Master in the Funding and Finance Technology division of Morgan Stanley in London. I joined the firm in 2016 through its Return to Work programme, and since then I've led agile teams across a range of projects, most recently the re-architecture of our core finance systems.

Most of the job is getting complex, interdependent systems and processes delivered on time while keeping the people who rely on them engaged. On core infrastructure platforms in particular there can be a real gap between end users and developers, and a lot of my work is closing it.

## How I got here

I started my career with a PhD in Chemistry, then spent two years in Japan researching liquid crystals at the Osaka National Research Institute.

Over time I found my niche in more client-focused and business development roles, working with clients and developers to deliver what the business needed. Before my career break, that meant five years at a boutique software house providing market data and analytics platforms to asset managers, hedge funds and banks.

The full list is on the [experience](/experience/) page.

## Outside work

I'm a keen sportswoman and have spent a good amount of time climbing, playing squash and running ultramarathons, until injury caught up with me. These days my two children and a bicycle keep me entertained, out in the rolling countryside of Buckinghamshire.
