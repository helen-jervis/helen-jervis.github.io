From 2012 to 2016 I took four years out of the workplace while my children were small. I came back through the Morgan Stanley Return to Work programme, and I consider myself very fortunate to have come across it.

Having been out that long myself, I understand the challenges of returning to work, and I feel strongly about supporting other people who are making the same journey.

## What I do now

Within Morgan Stanley I'm a strong advocate of flexible, part-time working based around school hours. I also act as a mentor to other returners, to make the transition back into the workplace a little easier than it might otherwise be.

WeAreTechWomen has also published [a profile of me](https://wearetechwomen.com/portfolio/helen-jervis-architect-scrum-master-morgan-stanley/).
